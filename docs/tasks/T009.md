## Schema and security

Five RLS-protected tables store Starter prices, immutable entitlement periods and a permanent first-100 trial ledger. First approval allocates six calendar months; ownership changes do not reset eligibility. A serialized subscription-row update enforces three active offers per property. Browser grants remain absent until T011; invoker functions have fixed search paths and private data stays unexposed. Paid activation is disabled by constraint. No payment processing, autocharge or paid infrastructure was introduced. See the [schema guide](../database/T009_ENTITLEMENTS.md) for constraints, privileges and expiry/read requirements.
