# T036 — Duplicate properties and ownership claims

Registration checks for a matching Google Maps URL or at least two matching signals among normalized name, street address, and business contact in the same district. A shared business number by itself is allowed. Matching applicants can explain their authority and submit a private ownership claim. The partner dashboard links to the claimant’s own status and the approved property after transfer.

Platform administrators can open **Ownership claims** from the admin overview. They review the submitted details and match signals, select the existing property, confirm independent verification of the claimant’s authority, and record evidence and a decision message. Approval transfers the existing property. The public profile, offers, trial, and property history stay with that property. Former staff assignments and unused invitations for that property are cleared. An in-progress verification request is withdrawn, and an unverified property receives a new initial review for the new owner.

## Security

Claim submissions require a verified account and use the existing registration request limits. Applicants receive a generic match result without candidate identifiers, ownership details, or internal evidence. Claims and candidates can only be reviewed by platform administrators. The database records the claimant, prior owner, reviewer, and decision. Approval locks and rechecks the claim, property, and owner assignment before changing access. Competing claims against the old owner assignment become stale.
