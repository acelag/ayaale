# T044 — Trial reminders and monthly partner performance

An active Starter trial queues a reminder for the verified property owner 30, 7, and 1 Sri Lanka calendar days before expiry. Each milestone can be queued once per property, including after a transfer. The message shows the expiry date and links to the subscription page. The email worker rechecks the trial and recipient before delivery.

During the first seven days of each Sri Lanka calendar month, a verified property with a previously published offer can receive one summary of the preceding month. The summary includes recorded offer views, WhatsApp clicks, and call clicks for that property and links to its analytics page. The totals exclude flagged events. When the offer-view endpoint is unavailable and the period has no recorded views, the email shows views as unavailable. The summary includes aggregate counts only.

## Files and migration

- `supabase/migrations/20261106174831_t044_partner_lifecycle_emails.sql` adds the scheduled queue producer, its unique milestone and month keys, and eligibility checks for the T043 worker.
- `supabase/operations/t044_development_schedule.sql` installs the free development schedule. `scripts/run-offer-lifecycle.mjs partner-emails` runs the job once for Render.
- `lib/email/transactional.mjs` links recipients to subscription and analytics. `lib/database.types.ts` records the new API signature.
- `tests/database/partner-lifecycle-emails.test.mjs` and the email unit tests cover milestones, month boundaries, recipient scope, suppression, and template links.

## Security

Only the trusted worker can queue these messages. The public job uses database time. Confirmed, unblocked owners are selected from the current property membership. The T043 worker checks ownership, trial eligibility, and the summary period again before delivery. No raw analytics events, contact details, or recipient email addresses are included in the notification body.
