# T033 — Partner workflows on phones

Property status groups the available workflows into separate touch targets. The property switcher gives the select room to shrink beside its Go button. Partner form controls use a font size of at least 16 CSS pixels, and the native upload picker has a 44-pixel target. Primary form actions use the available width on narrow screens.

The offer wizard keeps every step directly reachable. Back and Save draft share a row, with the primary action on its own row when space is tight. After a validation response, focus moves to the error above the relevant step. Normal step changes focus the step legend. Analytics labels are rendered at a readable text size independent of the chart’s width.

## Files

- `app/globals.css` and `app/partner/layout.tsx` scope the control sizing and spacing to partner screens.
- `app/partner/properties/[id]/page.tsx` and `components/partner/property-switcher.tsx` lay out property navigation and switching.
- `components/partner/offer-wizard.tsx` and `components/partner/property-analytics.tsx` improve step navigation, error recovery, and chart readability.
- `components/partner/auth-form.tsx`, `registration-form.tsx`, `registration-photo.tsx`, `property-profile-form.tsx`, `profile-photos.tsx`, and `team-management.tsx` adjust phone form and photo controls.
- `tests/e2e/partner-phone.spec.ts`, `offer-wizard.spec.ts`, and `partner-registration.spec.ts` cover phone route reflow, touch targets, validation focus, and photo selection.

## Schema and security

This is a presentation and interaction change. It uses the existing authentication, membership, upload, and offer validation paths. Owner-only links retain their role checks. The task requires no database migration.
