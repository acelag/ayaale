## Key finding

Ayaale does not verify customer booking/payment/attendance. Inbox control, a contact click, partner confirmation or business verification cannot independently support a “verified customer” claim. The proposed future pilot combines confirmed contact with human assessment of minimal redacted experience evidence and fair alternatives; evidence can still be forged and must not be described as a guarantee. Retaining the existing external reviews link is the recommended V1 decision.
