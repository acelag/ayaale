## Behavior and boundaries

Header has a home link, exact search placeholder “Search destinations, hotels, or offers.” and “List Your Property.” It is sticky and reflows to two rows on phones. The listing CTA has a real in-page destination explaining registration is coming later; no nonexistent registration route or form is presented.

All Offers, Stays, Dining and Day Outs are native URL-driven category links with aria-current, not ARIA tabs pretending to control panels. Search uses a labelled native GET form, preserves category, survives reload and works without JavaScript. Category links preserve the query; clear removes both. Unknown categories fall back to All Offers; repeated/non-string query values are ignored and query display is limited to 120 characters.

The page filters only four fictional in-memory presentation fixtures by category and text. These are not database listing queries or a production search implementation. The preview banner, sample property names and illustration labels are visible; the example verification badge is explicitly marked as an example. Card title links lead to the explanation of sample content. No fake provider/contact/detail route, booking activity, live availability, real verification or fabricated live listing is implied.

Cards show one dimensioned thumbnail, category/title, sample property/location, LKR price/unit, optional price comparison, sample guest coverage and brief inclusions. Prices use explicit en-LK formatting with up to two decimals. Fixture discounts are conservative example values; T016's approved-data adapter must use database-computed approved prices/discounts rather than trusting arbitrary client content. Optional verification copy explains identity/authorization checks do not guarantee quality or availability. Local authored SVGs require no remote image host or third-party request.

Live database search/feed, newest-first/fair ordering, 12-item pagination and Load More remain T016. Filters/sort/bottom sheets remain T017. Offer detail/contact workflows remain T018/T019; partner signup remains T024. No anonymous database grant or private Storage exposure was opened. T015 does not authorize those later tasks.

## Accessibility, responsive behavior and SEO

Server-rendered heading/content, one main/H1, skip link and meaningful native anchors are preserved. Persistent search label, keyboard focus, readable price units, decorative icon semantics and explicit empty/reset states are included. The skip destination has scroll clearance beneath the sticky header, with a browser assertion that the H1 stays visible after skipping. Thumbnail dimensions reserve layout space; thumbnails use Next Image and lazy loading with appropriate sizes. Cards remain horizontal at 320px, and two feed columns appear at 1024px. Touch targets are at least 44px for search, category and principal links. No motion was introduced beyond existing reduced-motion-aware controls.

The inherited noindex/nofollow remains intentional for fictional development content. No production canonicals, structured data, sitemap, tracking or legal pages were added early. Query text is escaped by React, encoded in generated navigation URLs, and never inserted as HTML or SQL. No auth/provider key is needed to render/search this preview.
