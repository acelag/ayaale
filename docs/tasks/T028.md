# T028 — Five-step offer wizard

From a property’s status page, owners and assigned staff can create or reopen a draft. The wizard covers basics, price and inclusions, dates and terms, photos, and private card and detail previews. Stays, Dining and Day Outs have their own fields. The pricing step previews the discount using the same two-decimal truncation as PostgreSQL. Publication times use Sri Lanka time. Redemption dates are separate calendar dates.

A draft can be saved from any step. Submission requires a verified property, an approved business phone or WhatsApp number, complete offer details and a cover photo. Partners can upload optimized photos through the existing private pipeline and add up to 11 extra gallery photos. The gallery is attached to the review revision. Public gallery delivery follows the approval workflow. The current wizard requires JavaScript.

## Files and migration

- `app/partner/properties/[id]/offer/page.tsx` loads the wizard, draft links and submission confirmation. The property status page links to it.
- `components/partner/offer-wizard.tsx`, `lib/partner/offer-wizard.ts` and `lib/partner/offer-actions.ts` implement the steps, field validation, preview and submission. The wizard and property editor share the private photo asset loader.
- `supabase/migrations/20261105172225_t028_offer_wizard.sql` adds private draft and revision gallery tables with property-scoped read and save functions. It is applied to free development Supabase. `lib/database.types.ts` includes the generated signatures.
- `tests/database/offer-wizard.test.mjs`, `tests/e2e/offer-wizard.spec.ts` and the partner browser fixture cover draft persistence, review submission and access.

## Security and review

Drafts use a private partial form so an incomplete offer does not need placeholder prices or dates. The database checks current membership on every read and write, rejects cross-property photos and stale writes, and handles repeated delivery of the same submission. Submission creates the offer, its first review revision and gallery in one transaction. The resulting offer is pending review and excluded from discovery. Photo previews use the existing protected route and short-lived links.
