# T042 — Offer expiry and inactive business reminders

The reminder job queues an alert for the verified owner of a live offer when its publication or redemption deadline is within 72 hours. The redemption date runs through the end of that day in Sri Lanka. The message states the scheduled offer expiry. Trial expiry has its own reminder task.

A business qualifies for an inactivity reminder once it has been verified for 30 days, has no live offers, and has had no offer go live or receive a live revision in the last 30 days. Each business gets one reminder per inactive period. Publishing again can start a new period. Ownership transfers and job retries do not reset reminders already queued for that period or deadline.

## Files and migration

- `supabase/migrations/20261106172318_t042_offer_and_inactivity_reminders.sql` adds a restricted queueing function and a unique index for T042 reminder keys on the existing notification table.
- `supabase/operations/t042_development_schedule.sql` schedules the job every 15 minutes in free development.
- `scripts/run-offer-lifecycle.mjs reminders` runs the job once for Render. `lib/database.types.ts` records the new API signature.
- `tests/database/offer-reminders.test.mjs` covers eligibility, repeat runs, transfers, and a new period after publication.

## Security

The job selects confirmed, unblocked owners and writes to the private notification queue. Anonymous users and authenticated browser sessions cannot call the job. The service command logs only totals. Delivery should recheck current ownership and eligibility before sending queued messages, since a listing or owner can change after queueing. T042 queues notifications. Email delivery and the partner notification interface are separate tasks.
