## Behavior and security

Auth actions have canonical-email/global quotas, generic messages and a signup honeypot. Photo upload/read routes have actor/global limits and upload property limits, 429/Retry-After and fail-closed configuration/database handling. Input streams time out after 20 seconds; decoder work has a two-slot per-process bound and per-operation timeout. HMAC subject hashes avoid persisting raw addresses/IPs in budgets.

Successful sensitive row changes are audited in their own transaction without copying row payloads. Auth attempts use service-only events. Database owners remain trusted, service-write actors may be null, and denied requests are not expanded into unbounded audit rows. No CAPTCHA integration, raw IP tracking, email provider change, generic client mutation grant or future form was added. See the [controls guide](../security/T014_CONTROLS.md) for exact limits, coverage, retention and direct-Auth endpoint limitations.
