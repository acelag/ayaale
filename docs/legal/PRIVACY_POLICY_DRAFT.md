You can browse offers without a customer account. Report email/comment fields are optional. Required partner fields are identified when collected; without information needed for verification, we may be unable to approve a listing. We may obtain business information from public sources, the business or an authorized representative, and record the source when appropriate.

Do not send payment-card details, identity documents, health information or information about children through public forms. If verification evidence is necessary, we will explain what is needed and use an appropriate private channel; unnecessary details should be redacted.

## How we justify processing

We use information for the service you request, account/listing administration, security and legitimate operational needs, and legal obligations where applicable. Newsletter marketing is optional and based on your confirmed choice. Optional analytics requiring consent will wait for that consent. Accepting website or listing terms does not also subscribe you to marketing.

The legal basis for each activity depends on the applicable law and circumstances. We will explain any additional use before collecting information for that purpose and obtain a new choice where required.

## What becomes public and who receives information

Approved business names, descriptions, locations, photos, selected business contacts and offers are public and may be indexed, shared or cached by others. Representatives should use business contact details they are authorized to publish. Login emails and verification evidence are not automatically public.

Authorized Ayaale personnel and service providers receive information only for their assigned work. Property dashboards receive aggregated view/contact-click statistics, not subscriber lists or visitor identities. Reporter contact details are not exposed to property staff. To investigate a report, we may share a relevant, redacted summary with the business. Some circumstances may require disclosure to competent authorities or advisers; we disclose only what is appropriate or legally required.

We do not sell subscriber lists or personal information. We do not provide businesses with newsletter subscribers' email addresses. We may disclose relevant records to satisfy a lawful request, protect people or investigate misuse, subject to applicable safeguards.

Verification, ownership claims and complaint decisions are reviewed by people. Report counts alone do not automatically suspend a business. Routine date-based scheduling and expiry may be automated, but do not amount to a guarantee that an offer remains available.

## Contact links and external services

WhatsApp, Call, Maps, Google reviews and other external links open another service or device application. When you choose one, that service may receive your information under its own terms. A WhatsApp draft includes the offer title and URL; you can review it before sending.

Ayaale counts a contact-button click, not a confirmed enquiry or booking. Our click measurement does not give us access to your WhatsApp messages, call contents or provider booking records.

## Cookies and analytics

Essential technologies support account security and functionality when those features are available. Our planned optional Google Analytics 4 integration measures site usage/acquisition; it is separate from provider-specific aggregate reporting. Optional tracking will not be enabled before applicable notices and choices are implemented. You can decline optional analytics and continue browsing, and later change your choice through [[PRIVACY_CHOICES_LOCATION]].

We do not send report text, verification documents, account email addresses or other direct identifiers to analytics. Our newsletter may measure link clicks; we do not plan newsletter open-tracking pixels at launch. Unsubscribing stops future marketing sends; it does not undo a link request already made.

## How long we keep information

We keep information only for its purpose and applicable recordkeeping needs. The following are proposed launch limits, subject to approval and verified deletion controls:

| Category                                       | Proposed period / rule                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Security/access logs                           | Normally 30 days; selected incident records may need longer                                                              |
| Identifiable offer/contact events              | Up to 90 days, then delete or irreversibly aggregate                                                                     |
| Non-identifying aggregate performance          | Up to 24 months, provided it cannot reasonably identify individuals                                                      |
| Unconfirmed newsletter requests                | Delete after 7 days unless confirmation or a documented abuse investigation requires action                              |
| Active newsletter address                      | Until withdrawal, removal or the approved inactivity process                                                             |
| Suppression/consent evidence                   | Minimum information needed to prevent unwanted sends and demonstrate choices; review necessity annually                  |
| Verification evidence                          | Review 90 days after the decision/claim closes; remove documents no longer needed and retain a minimized decision record |
| Partner account and moderation/support records | During the relationship/case and normally up to 24 months after closure, subject to the exceptions below                 |

A business profile may remain public without active offers. Historical trial eligibility may require a minimized property-level record so ownership changes do not create a second trial; it should not retain unnecessary former-staff personal information.

Lawful retention duties, unresolved investigations, disputes or legal holds may require limited records to be kept longer. Such exceptions must be documented and reviewed. Backup copies expire through [[BACKUP_RETENTION_AND_DELETION_PROCESS]]; a deletion must be reapplied if a backup is restored. We do not promise immediate erasure from every backup or third-party cache.

## Your choices and requests

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter through the link in each marketing email without logging in. You can also contact [[PRIVACY_EMAIL]] to ask about access, correction, deletion, objection or withdrawal of consent. We may need proportionate identity verification to protect your information. We will explain any lawful reason a request cannot be fulfilled in full.

Any statutory rights, time limits and regulator procedures apply only when the relevant provisions are in force and applicable to you. This policy does not claim that every PDPA right is already operational. For current information about the Sri Lankan regulator, see the [Data Protection Authority](https://www.dpa.gov.lk/). Our own handling commitments do not remove rights available under other applicable law.

## Security, children and changes

We use access controls and other safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. No online system can guarantee complete security. We do not intentionally collect children's personal information through newsletter or partner signup. Child guest counts/age bands in offers describe package eligibility; they are not a request for children's identities. A parent/guardian who believes information was submitted about a child can contact [[PRIVACY_EMAIL]].

Material policy changes will be dated and explained. Where a change requires a fresh choice, we will ask for it before applying that new use. You can request this policy in an accessible format at [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]].
