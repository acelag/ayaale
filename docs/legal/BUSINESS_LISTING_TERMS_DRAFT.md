## Parties, authority and accounts

These proposed terms apply between [[OPERATOR_LEGAL_NAME]], [[OPERATOR_ADDRESS]] (“Ayaale”), and the business identified in the approved listing. The person accepting must have authority to bind that business and must provide accurate business/representative details. If that authority is absent, do not submit an acceptance on the business's behalf. Contact [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]] for assistance.

One property may have one owner/admin and up to two staff accounts. A person may be assigned to multiple properties. Staff act only for assigned properties and cannot manage subscriptions or teams. Use individual verified-email accounts, keep access secure, and remove access when authority/employment changes. An account role is not proof of legal business ownership.

## Eligible listings and verification

V1 covers Stays, Dining and Day Outs. Homestays are not eligible for the Stays category. Businesses must hold any authorizations/licences required for their actual activities; Ayaale verification does not replace these.

We check business existence, location and applicant authorization, using independently confirmed contact information and proportionate evidence where needed. Verification may be pending, require more information, be approved or rejected; a previously approved business may be suspended. Approval is not guaranteed by registration or payment.

You may draft offers while verification is pending, but submission requires verification. Critical changes such as name, address, ownership or primary contact require review. Approved public information may remain visible while changes are considered where safe. Do not create duplicate listings to bypass review, trial rules or moderation. Competing ownership claims require independent verification; current-owner access is not transferred merely because another person submits a claim.

## Accurate offers and provider responsibilities

You are responsible for lawful, truthful content and the services you provide. Before submitting an offer, verify the promotional price and unit, genuine optional comparison price, guest/child-age/nights coverage, inclusions, separate publication/redemption dates, mandatory taxes/charges, exclusions, restrictions and contact channels. Do not advertise an inflated original price or imply an inclusion that is only a general property amenity.

You must have authority to publish each photo, logo and description and permission to publish personal/business contact details. Avoid photos identifying guests or children without appropriate authorization. Do not submit private verification evidence as public gallery content.

Every new offer and public content edit requires manual Ayaale review. A draft, submitted offer or approved scheduled offer is not necessarily active. Review is not a guarantee of service quality or a waiver of your responsibility for accuracy.

Pause or end an offer immediately when it cannot be honored, and respond appropriately to customers who already contacted you. Public content changes and date extensions require review. An unchanged, approved paused offer may be re-enabled only when its dates, subscription and active-offer capacity permit. Ayaale may hide unsafe, misleading or otherwise problematic content while investigating.

Customers contact and contract with you directly. You must explain your booking, payment, cancellation/refund and complaint arrangements before taking a commitment, comply with applicable law, and honor agreed terms. Ayaale provides no customer checkout, escrow or payment guarantee in V1.

## Plans and first-100 trial

The proposed Starter plan is per property, with up to three simultaneously active offers and unlimited drafts. The V1 schedule is LKR 1,500 per month, LKR 8,000 per six months, or LKR 15,000 per year. [[SUBSCRIPTION_TAX_TREATMENT]] must be resolved and displayed before any paid offer is made. These figures do not authorize payment collection through this draft.

The first 100 approved, verified properties are eligible for a six-month Starter trial. Places are allocated at approval, not registration, and the trial starts at approval time. Each property can receive it once; ownership transfers do not restart it. Genuine separate properties are verified separately. We must display actual entitlement and expiry in the account; do not promise a place before allocation.

There is no automatic charge at trial expiry. Without an eligible paid subscription, offers become inactive; drafts, history, analytics and the verified public profile remain, subject to lawful moderation and retention rules. Subscription expiry alone is not account deletion.

**Payments are disabled at launch preparation.** Ayaale will not collect subscription payments until business registration, banking and the payment provider are ready and paid terms are approved. Any later payment feature requires clearly disclosed total charges, renewal/cancellation/refund rules and an affirmative agreement before charging. This draft does not create automatic renewal or store payment credentials. Price changes require advance disclosure and do not retroactively alter an agreed paid period.

## Content licence and data

You retain ownership of content you supply. You grant Ayaale a non-exclusive licence, for operating and promoting the Ayaale listing service, to store, resize, format and display approved content on Ayaale and in its own offer newsletters/social sharing. This does not authorize unrelated resale or misleading edits. Promotion must use accurate, authorized content; expired offers must not be promoted as active. Historic legal/audit records and third-party caches may remain where appropriate after removal.

Each party must handle personal information lawfully. Use business contact details appropriately; do not upload customer lists, payment records or unnecessary identity documents. Ayaale's processing is described in its privacy policy. A business is responsible for the customer information it receives directly. These listing terms do not silently appoint either party as a processor for all of the other's activities; any processor arrangement needed must be separately assessed/documented.

Dashboards report views and WhatsApp/Call clicks, not confirmed bookings, sales or guaranteed enquiries. Estimates may exclude suspected bots and rapid repeats. Ayaale does not promise a minimum number of views, customers, ranking position or commercial results. Reporter contact details and newsletter subscribers' personal data are not available to property staff.

## Moderation, account closure and disputes

We may request evidence/corrections, decline content, restrict access or suspend a listing for misuse, false authority, legal concerns or serious safety issues. We assess complaints manually; raw report counts alone do not decide suspension. Where appropriate, we explain the reason and allow a response through [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]]. Urgent temporary restrictions may precede that response.

You can request closure or challenge an access/ownership decision at [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]]. We may verify authority and resolve active ownership disputes before changing control. Public removal, account closure and data erasure are distinct actions; minimum records may remain for lawful purposes and trial-integrity checks. Proposed closure/export steps must be finalized before partner launch, without implying a new dashboard feature in this task.

Each party remains responsible for its own conduct. Nothing here excludes responsibility that cannot lawfully be excluded or removes mandatory rights. No unlimited indemnity or blanket waiver is included. Sri Lankan governing law and competent dispute forums are proposed, subject to mandatory law and legal review. Material changes to these terms will be notified; a fresh acceptance is required where the nature of the change calls for one. Accepted versions must remain accessible.
